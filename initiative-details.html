Environment • Water

Sustainable Millet Cultivation & Water Stewardship

Championing rainfed, low-input dryland farming that consumes 70% less water than rice while regenerating fragile topsoils across semi-arid belts.

The Mission

Why This Initiative Matters

Commercial agriculture accounts for over 70% of global freshwater withdrawals. By revitalizing indigenous millet crops across arid and semi-arid dryland belts, we eliminate artificial flood irrigation. Our partner fields rely predominantly on natural seasonal rainfall, preserving precious subterranean aquifers for future generations while rebuilding microbial soil organic matter.
Geographical Scope:
Operating across 14 rural clusters in semi-arid agrarian belts, directly engaging over 400 grower households.

Key Strategic Milestones

  • Transition 1,200+ hectares of semi-arid farmland to certified zero-chemical organic cultivation.
  • Conserve over 400 million liters of groundwater annually through rainfed crop selection.
  • Implement contour rainwater harvesting trenches across 100% of partner cooperative acreage.
Operations

How We Execute in the Field

Our agronomists work side by side with village cooperatives from seed selection to post-harvest threshing.

01

Seed Distribution

Free organic heirloom seeds distributed before seasonal monsoon showers, eliminating farmer seed debt.

02

Organic Bio-Inputs

Training on traditional neem, cow dung slurry, and botanical pest repellents, eliminating expensive petrochemicals.

03

Direct Gate Procurement

Clean mechanical thresher collection centers purchase grain immediately at harvest, paying fair guaranteed premiums.
