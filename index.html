Nutritional Science

Why Millets Belong in Your Modern Kitchen

Modern diets heavy in ultra-processed wheat flour and white rice often leave us feeling tired and spike our blood sugar. Millets offer an extraordinary density of slow-digesting complex carbohydrates, plant protein, and vital micronutrients.

Steady Blood Glucose & Satiety

High amylose and prebiotic fiber ensure a slow, sustained glucose release (GI under 55), preventing post-meal energy crashes.

Naturally 100% Gluten-Free

Gentle on sensitive digestive tracts, eliminating bloating without the need for artificial binders or gums.

Superior Bone Fortification

Ragi provides 344mg of calcium per 100g—more than thirty times that of polished rice, supporting bone density naturally.

View Detailed Nutritional Comparison

Water-Smart Crops

Millets thrive in rainfed soils with 70% less irrigation than rice, preserving fragile subterranean aquifers for future generations.

Zero Chemical Dependency

Indigenous landraces possess natural evolutionary pest resilience, flourishing without toxic synthetic pesticides or fertilizers.

Rich in Polyphenols

Abundant in tannins, phytosterols, and ferulic acid that actively combat oxidative stress and support arterial elasticity.

Gut Microbiome Friendly

Fermentable dietary fibers nourish beneficial probiotic Bifidobacteria, promoting healthy digestion and smooth bowel transit.

From Rainfed Soil to Your Table

We control every link of the supply chain—from non-GMO heirloom seeds to clean solar-powered processing and compostable packaging.

01

Regenerative Sourcing

Direct fair-trade partnerships with 400+ smallholder farmers across semi-arid dryland belts, paid 25% above statutory market rates.

02

Natural Germination

48-hour controlled sprouting activates vital phytase enzymes, breaking down phytic acid to maximize bioavailable calcium and iron.

03

Slow Stone Milling

Traditional slow stone chakkis run under 32°C to prevent thermal oxidation, preserving delicate vitamins, germ oils, and natural grain aroma.

04

Airtight Eco-Packs

Sealed in nitrogen-flushed, unbleached kraft biodegradable pouches that lock out ambient moisture without harmful preservatives.
